# Licensing FAQ

Short answers to the questions people actually ask. The binding text is
[LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md).

### Can I use this in my own game?

Yes, including commercially. Free to play, free to build on.

### Do I have to credit you?

Yes — that is the one condition. Credit **Dr. Sohil Momin** as the original creator
of the Neon Engine. A line in your credits screen, README or about page is enough.

### Can I sell a game I made with it?

Yes. The credit requirement still applies.

### Can I modify the engine itself?

Yes, and you may redistribute your modified version — with the credit intact.

### Can I remove the author credit from inside the game?

No. That is the one thing the licence prohibits.

### What if I fork it and publish my fork?

Fine, as long as your fork also credits Dr. Sohil Momin and does not present the
original engine or the original games as your own work.

### Does the credit have to be visible to players?

No. A line in your repository or documentation satisfies it.

### What about three.js?

`three.module.js` and `BufferGeometryUtils.js` are PolyForm Noncommercial-licensed and keep their own
terms. This licence covers the Neon Engine and its games only.

### Is there a warranty?

No. Provided "as is", with no warranty of any kind.

### Can I use the name "Neon Engine" for my fork?

Please don't imply it is the original. Rename your fork, or make it clear it is a
derivative.

### Can I use the assets?

There are no asset files — every texture, mesh and sound is generated in code. So
there is nothing separate to license.
